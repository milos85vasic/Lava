## Smoke checklist for Stage 1 testers

Beyond cold launch:

1. Install the debug APK from Firebase invite.
2. Cold-launch — confirm Welcome screen renders.
3. Tap "Get Started" — confirm the NEW **"Choose your API"** screen appears (this is the new ApiSelection step).
4. Confirm the discovery spinner appears + status text "Searching for APIs on your network…".
5. If your LAN has a `lava-api-go` running on the discoverable mDNS service type (`_lava-api._tcp` for prod / `_lava-api-dev._tcp` for dev), the API should appear in the list within 5 seconds.
6. Tap an API → confirm a per-row spinner appears (probe in flight).
7. On probe success → confirm advance to "Pick your providers" screen.
8. Under each provider, confirm subtitle reads "None" / "Form Login" / "Api Key" / "Captcha Login" / "Oauth" — NOT raw enum names with underscores.
9. (Optional) If no APIs discovered: confirm "No APIs discovered on your network." status + "Search again" button.
